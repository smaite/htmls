Part 02 · The method

How the Lessons Teach

No. 01

Explain, don't lecture

Every rule comes with the reason it exists. Rules you understand are rules you keep; rules you memorize are rules you forget by Friday.

No. 02

Show the bad twin

Each good example sits next to a bad one, annotated with exactly what went wrong. You learn to spot the difference with your own eyes — which is the whole game.

No. 03

Drill until it sticks

Checklists you can tick, exercises you can click, and one gold-standard test throughout: could a stranger follow your writing and succeed?
